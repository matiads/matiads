# Hey, I'm Matias 👋

I work in paid media and spend a lot of my free time exploring AI, automation, and self-hosted software.

I like figuring out how things work, connecting tools, running my own infrastructure, and using AI to build things that solve real problems.

I'm not a traditional developer. I come from marketing, and most of what I build starts with a problem I want to solve or something I'm curious about.

## What I'm into

- **AI & automation** — AI agents, coding assistants, APIs, n8n, and experimenting with different models.
- **Self-hosting & infrastructure** — Linux, Docker, Proxmox, VPS, bare metal, and homelabs.
- **Paid media** — Meta Ads, Google Ads, campaign workflows, and performance marketing.
- **Building things** — Internal tools, integrations, automations, and open-source experiments, usually with a little help from AI.
